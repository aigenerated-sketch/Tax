## Test strategy added

- `auditRegression.test.ts` — one block per finding F1–F7. All 10 tests failed against `104f591`
  (output in `docs/audit/regression-tests-before-fix.txt`).
- `independentReference.test.ts` — a separately written calculator with 2026/27 figures typed from
  GOV.UK / gov.scot (not imported from `src/rules`) compared to the penny with the engine over 185
  salaries × {England, Scotland} × {no contribution, sacrifice, net pay, SIPP}; monotonicity sweeps for
  both years and jurisdictions; brute-force allowance-allocation check; optimiser validity
  (`ANI(required) ≤ target < ANI(required − 1p)`) across 8 profiles × 4 methods incl. 2025/26, Gift Aid,
  savings/dividends and Scotland.
- `savingsAndAllowance.test.ts` — hand-worked PSA, starting-rate and annual allowance boundary cases.
- UI tests for F2–F5.
