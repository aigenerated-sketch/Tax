## Uncertain, assumed or not yet verified

**U1. Annual, not per-period, payroll.** Income Tax and NI are calculated on annual totals. PAYE uses cumulative tax codes and NI is non-cumulative per pay period. *Handling:* Always shown as an assumption; bonus months can differ.

**U2. Scottish relief-at-source and Gift Aid mechanics.** GOV.UK states the extra relief Scottish taxpayers receive (none at 19%, 1% at 21%, 22% at 42%, 25% at 45%, 28% at 48%) but not the exact mechanism. The engine extends the Scottish basic band and every band above it, which reproduces those rates. *Handling:* Labelled as an estimate for Scottish users; tested against the GOV.UK rates.

**U3. Personal savings allowance for Scottish taxpayers (resolved).** Resolved on audit: ITA 2007 s12B(8) determines the band as if the person were not a Scottish taxpayer, so UK thresholds are correct. *Handling:* Information note only.

**U4. Personal allowance taper rounding.** Statute reduces the allowance by one-half of the excess; GOV.UK says £1 for every £2. The engine removes £1 per complete £2 after truncating ANI to whole pounds. *Handling:* Maximum difference 50p of allowance (≤ 22.5p of tax). Documented and tested at £100,001 / £100,002.

**U5. Gift Aid 4× test.** GOV.UK: donations must not exceed 4 times the tax paid. Capital Gains Tax is not modelled, so the check uses Income Tax only. *Handling:* Warning only; the shortfall is not added to the figures.

**U6. Annual allowance inputs.** Lump-sum death benefits are not modelled; the DB pension input must be supplied by the user from the scheme statement (its calculation — opening/closing values, CPI uplift — is not modelled); DB member contributions are now a separate input (F13); carry-forward eligibility (membership, year order) is not checked; contributions after age 75 and scheme refunds of excess contributions are not modelled. Gift Aid treatment resolved on audit: s228ZA does not deduct it. *Handling:* Annual allowance result labelled "not verified" unless carry-forward is entered, MPAA status is known, and no DB scheme is involved.

**U7. Annual allowance charge rate (Scotland).** Fixed on audit (F11): the excess is stacked on reduced net income after the personal allowance, at main rates, and the Scottish starter rate is excluded. Remaining uncertainty: PTM056110’s Scottish table (46% / 41%) pre-dates the advanced rate, so the engine applies the current Scottish bands above the basic band; HMRC has not published an updated worked table that was found. *Handling:* Charge shown as "roughly"; documented.

**U8. Childcare outside England and edge cases.** Funded-hours schemes in Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland are not assessed. Child age is a band, not a date. Minimum-income exceptions (carers, some benefits, new self-employed) are not modelled. *Handling:* Shown as "Not assessed" or "Eligibility not confirmed"; exceptions named in the failure text.

**U9. Student loans.** One undergraduate plan plus optional postgraduate loan; Self Assessment repayments on unearned income are not modelled. *Handling:* Assumption shown when a loan is selected.

**U10. Scottish Rate Resolution.** Band figures are official (Scottish Government and HMRC). Approval of the 2026-27 Scottish Rate Resolution on 19 February 2026 was confirmed from the Scottish Parliament motion page and CIOT, not from a Scottish Government page. *Handling:* No effect on figures; HMRC publishes the same bands as in force.

**U11. Build-brief discrepancy (resolved).** The brief described the Scottish intermediate band as "the next £12,570". The published band is £16,957–£31,092 of taxable income (£14,136 wide). *Handling:* Official figure used and tested.

**U13. Personal allowance allocation search.** ITA s25(2) requires the allocation giving the greatest reduction. The engine evaluates every priority order of non-savings, savings and dividends. A brute-force test of fractional splits on a grid of mixed incomes found no cheaper split, but this is not a proof for every input. *Handling:* Documented; in every tested case the result equals non-savings first.

**U12. 2025/26 rules.** The 2025/26 rule set (provided for comparison) was checked against the GOV.UK 2025-26 employer page and NMW rates, but has no rule-by-rule register. *Handling:* Default year is 2026/27.
